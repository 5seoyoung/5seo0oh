Seoyoung Oh

Medical AI · Seoul, Korea

Hi, I’m Seoyoung Oh. I’m a researcher in the Department of Transdisciplinary Medicine at Seoul National University Hospital, where I’ve worked since December 2025. I believe medicine is the most meaningful application of AI and data science because of its direct impact on human life and quality of life. Rather than applying AI across various domains, I aspire to dedicate my research to medicine, developing deep expertise in this field and addressing real-world healthcare challenges. I am particularly interested in building medical AI that goes beyond predictive performance to deliver trustworthy and explainable results that can be meaningfully applied in clinical practice.
