Onmom Coming soon

AI postpartum recovery platform, built with a student team from Kyung Hee University College of Medicine, Kyung Hee University Medical Center, and Kyung Hee University Hospital at Gangdong

Delivery is over, but recovery continues. Onmom turns fragmented medical information into a personalized recovery profile and guides mothers through the 84-day rehabilitation period that follows the first month of postpartum care: tailored rehabilitation exercises, postpartum depression screening, red flag safety alerts, and connections to nearby clinics. AI reads, organizes, and connects; every decision stays rule-based, so the app never diagnoses or prescribes. Built for iOS and Android.
